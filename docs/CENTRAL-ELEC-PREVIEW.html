Job status flow — one PRM job
PM job dueSame job, same due date as today. Shows in plant planner's calendar (all jobs) and electrical planners' calendar (only if it has ⚡ points).
→
Part 1 — either team firstGeneral tech answers M/C points → Stop.
or Electrical tech answers E points → Stop.
→
Waiting: Electrical / Waiting: GeneralNot finished. No report. Leaves the first technician's list.
→
Part 2Other team answers its points → Stop.
→
FinishedAll points done → PM checklist report made → plant planner accepts.
  • No electrical points on the machine → works exactly as today.
  • All points electrical (transformer, MDB, cap bank, MCC) → only the electrical team sees and does it.
  • PM on time → only if both parts finish inside the PM period.
  • Time credit → each part credits its own crew.
  • Electrical NG → CM job raised for the Central Electrical team.
  • Jobs open on 1 Oct → new rule applies even if already started. Jobs finished before 1 Oct → not changed.
