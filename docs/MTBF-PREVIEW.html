  • MTTR is untouched: repair hours ÷ breakdowns is sound as a fleet sum.
  • Machine availability keeps the fleet denominator on purpose, with its "2 machines below 90%" counter — the same pattern this MTBF card now follows.

One thing to decide
  • The word on the card. "Fleet MTBF" over a number built only from machines that failed needs its sub-line to say so — proposed: 5 of 157 machines failed · 9 breakdowns.
  • The window is short. 9 breakdowns over a month is below the 5-breakdown / 28-day stability gate already in the code, so the card should keep its "unstable" note on shorter ranges.
