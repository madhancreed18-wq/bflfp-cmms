One human, two lists“signs in as tech1”, “reports through sup1”, “no login linked” — the link between the two panels is written out as a sentence, so it can only be read one row at a time.
A third of the screen says nothingPreview sits empty until something is clicked, and it is empty most of the time.
Nothing lines upName, username, role and plant run together in prose. Two people with the same role cannot be compared without reading both lines word by word.

A Plant first, then one list of peoplerecommended

Pick the plant at the top; everything below belongs to it. Then one row per human — because a login and a person are the same person seen twice. The account becomes a column, so “who has no login”, “who shares tech1” and “who reports through sup1” are all answerable by looking down a column instead of reading sentences. Preview stops being a permanent empty third and opens only when you click someone.
