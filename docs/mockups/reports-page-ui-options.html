What the page does not do today

  • Two thirds of the screen is empty. Eight rows, then 500px of nothing — on the one page where you would want to actually read a report.
  • The Techs column is always 0. It reads people, which only the plan report fills — a daily report never sets it. A column of zeros teaches people to ignore columns.
  • The row already knows more than it shows. Every shelf row stores done_n · doing_n · accept_n · open_n · pm_n · cm_n. The table shows two of the six, so "51 jobs, 1 done, 24 not started" leaves 26 jobs unaccounted for on screen.
  • The tiles do not say what they are. 101 and 9 are the sums of the Jobs and Done columns below, but nothing says so, and they change when you switch month.
  • Two date columns that mostly agree. "For day" and "Issued" differ only when a report went out late — which is interesting, and invisible.
  • No coverage answer. "8 days covered" — out of how many working days? Aug 30 and Sept 6 have no report and the page cannot say so.

The list keeps its shape but earns its width: click a row and the document opens on the right instead of in a new tab. The wasted space becomes the reading pane.

What I would do

A now, with B's coverage strip on top of it. Option A spends data the rows already carry and turns the dead half of the screen into the document — that is the daily win, and it is the smallest change of the four. Option B answers the one question the page is asked at audit time and works as a thin month strip above the list rather than a separate screen. Option C is worth doing the day someone reads reports on a phone. Option D is right once the four tabs hold hundreds of documents — not yet.

Either way, drop the Techs column (always 0 on a daily report) and label the tiles with what they sum.

Data check — what each option needs

  • A · already stored. plan_reports holds done_n · doing_n · accept_n · open_n · pm_n · cm_n · jobs · creator · created_at · path. Status bar, late flag and tile labels need no server change; only the inline PDF pane is new wiring.
  • B needs the plant's working days — the Hours screen already holds them — plus a per-day "were any jobs closed" count. Small query.
  • C needs nothing new.
  • D needs one endpoint returning all four document kinds, and the gap query.
