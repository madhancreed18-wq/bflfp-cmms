B Signing on a PC

A mouse cannot draw a signature — it will look wrong however carefully you move it. The fix is not a better drawing box; it is to stop drawing at the moment of signing. Below: what a planner would see, and what it takes to build.

Now ตรวจรับงาน + เซ็นชื่อ / Approve + sign, on a PC

ตรวจรับงาน + เซ็นชื่อ

Approve + sign — ผู้ตรวจรับ เซ็นเพื่อรับงานที่เสร็จ

เซ็นในกรอบนี้ / sign here
Fine on a phone with a finger. On a PC it is a mouse scribble, and there are 30 PM sheets in a sitting.

Worth knowing before you choose. The app already records who accepted and when — approver_id and approved_at, on the job and in the audit trail. That is stronger evidence than any drawn line, and it is not affected by any of these options. The drawing exists for the printed form and for QA's eye.
