Putting it together

F for the list, E for the document, on one screen. The register styling from F costs almost nothing — every figure is already on the row — and it removes the dead Techs column and the puzzle of "51 jobs, 1 done". E is what fills the empty half: click a line, the statement opens beside it, and what you see is what prints.

G is a print button, not a screen — worth adding the day an auditor asks for the month. H belongs on the Repair forms and PM Reports tabs, where the photo and the two signatures are the record.

Combined with the earlier set: A + F + E is one coherent page — register list, statement preview, document styling — and only the inline preview is real new wiring.
