    What changed, and the new hold rule

    The hold rule you asked for

    Stage 1ConditionStage 2 shows
    HoldNo due date on the jobPending due date — the planner still owes this job a date
    HoldDue date set by the plannerWait for action — parked until the part or the supplier arrives

    Everything else in stage_for() stays as it is. Two code changes make it work: stage_for() in server/db.py takes due_date and branches on it for Hold and Paused; set_stage() reads that column and must also run when a due date is added or removed (update_job, plan_jobs, the assign board). stagePair() in static/index.html gets the same branch so old rows without stored stages still read right.

    Layout — what moved and why

    1. One header carries the identity. Job number, machine, Stage 1, Stage 2, type, priority and trade sit on one line instead of being scattered — the same pair the Jobs table shows, so the two screens agree.
    2. A facts strip replaces the four loose spans. Reported, Planned, Due, Assigned, Elapsed, Age — fixed places, monospaced figures, so the eye lands on the same spot every time.
    3. The hold reason gets a banner, not a hidden field. Today pending_reason is only visible on the planner list. Here it is the first thing on the page, with the action the job is waiting for.
    4. Progress rail. Reported → Assigned → In progress → Completed → Approved, with the real timestamps out of job_events. A held job shows a red break in the rail.
    5. Photos and signatures gain captions and times. The before/after pair says when each was taken; the three signature boxes say who owes which one and that the planner's unlocks the PDF download.
    6. Time log totals. Segments still list, but with a duration per row and a total, which is what the man-hour figure is built from.
    7. Planner action card is sticky. Due date and hold reason are editable from the job itself, so setting a date does not mean opening the re-plan dialog.

    Still to decide

    • Should Paused follow the same rule as Hold, or stay Assigned? Paused is a short break inside a shift; Hold is a wait on someone outside.
    • Does a due date that passes while the job is on hold flip Stage 2 to Overdue, or stay Wait for action and let the Overdue chip on the Jobs list carry it?
    • Should the app push a reminder to the planner while a job sits at Pending due date — and after how long?
