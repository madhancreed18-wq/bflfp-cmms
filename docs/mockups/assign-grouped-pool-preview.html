1 · The problem this solves

Today the pool is one flat list, newest-priority first, and every job is dragged on its own. On a 44-jobs-a-day plant that is 44 drags, and nothing on screen tells the planner that eight of them are all on floor 2 — which is exactly the grouping that keeps a crew in one area instead of walking the plant. The chips filter by type (PM · CM · BD), never by place.

What changed: the pool is grouped, each group header carries a tick box and a drag handle, and the tick boxes on the cards let a planner pick any mix by hand. The dark bar counts the selection and offers a one-press "add to crew" for phones and for people who dislike dragging. Everything else on the board is untouched.

3 · How it behaves

  • Group by — ชั้น (floor) by default, and ไลน์ (line) · แผนก (department) · กลุ่มสี PM (PM colour group) · ไม่จัดกลุ่ม (flat, today's behaviour) are one press away. The choice is remembered per browser.
  • Machines with no floor land in a group called ไม่ระบุชั้น / No floor at the bottom, never hidden.
  • Dragging one unselected card behaves exactly as it does today — one job moves. Nothing a planner already knows stops working.
  • Dragging a selected card drags the whole selection; the card shows a badge with how many are coming.
  • Dragging a group header drags every job in that group, whether or not the tick boxes are set.
  • The group tick box is three-state: empty, half (some picked), full.
  • Jobs already started stay locked, as now — a group drop simply skips them, and says how many it skipped.
  • The filter chips still apply: filter to PM, and a group drop takes only the PM jobs on that floor.
  • Undo — nothing is written until บันทึกและมอบหมาย (Save and assign), so a wrong group drop is dragged straight back to the pool.

4 · What it needs in the code

  • server/teams.py — the three job queries and _pm_due() return m.code, m.name only. They need floor, line, department, asset_group, pm_group_color as well. That is the whole server change: no new endpoint, no schema change.
  • static/index.html — asgDraw()'s pool section groups shown by the chosen key and renders a header per group; asgJobCard gains a tick box; asgDragStart carries a list of uids instead of one; asgDrop loops. The phone board gets the same selection bar, where it is worth more than on the desk.
  • No change to how jobs are stored — a group drop is still N ordinary assignments, so Save writes exactly what it writes today.

5 · Two things to decide

1. What is the default grouping? Floor is what you asked for, but your register also carries Line, Department and the PM colour group — and the colour group is the one already meant to keep a crew in one area.
→ Recommend: default ชั้น (floor) as you asked, with the others one press away. If floor turns out thin in the register, the switcher means nobody is stuck.
2. Should a group drop respect the crew's capacity? Dropping 8 jobs onto a crew of 2 is easy to do by accident.
→ Recommend: no hard limit, but the crew header turns amber past a threshold you set (say 6 jobs per person per day), so it is visible rather than forbidden. Say the word if you want a hard stop instead.

Worth knowing before you decide

This makes assigning faster, but it does not by itself even out the load between two people — everyone in a crew is on every job that crew holds, and the per-technician figures still credit only whoever pressed Start. Those are the two items from the last message; this one is independent of them.
