How this would work in the real app

  • Technician list comes from Admin → Users: role technician, active, same factory. No separate roster to maintain.
  • A team is a lead + helpers. Dropping a job on Team A writes lead_tech = the ★ member and helpers = the rest — exactly what POST /api/jobs/plan already accepts, so Save is one call per team and no schema change is needed for the assignment itself.
  • One technician, one team per day — once picked they're greyed out in the next "Add team" dialog, as you asked.
  • Save sets each job to Released and pushes the usual "งานใหม่ถึงคุณ" notification to every member of the team.
  • Jobs can be dragged team → team, or back to the jobs pane to unassign.
Three things to decide before I build it
  • Do teams persist? Rebuilt each day from scratch (simplest), or saved so tomorrow opens with the same teams ready to reuse — that needs one small table, plan_teams.
  • Jobs already having a technician (most of 10 Aug are assigned to สมชาย): should they appear pre-placed in that person's team, or sit in the pool until you drop them? Preview shows pre-placed.
  • Start/end time per team — worth adding to the team header, or leave times on the individual job?
