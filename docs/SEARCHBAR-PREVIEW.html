Search bar — one line, grows sideways

Working preview. Add filters and watch the bar widen; past its limit the extras fold into a dropdown. Nothing in the app has changed.

1 · What you asked to change

  • The bar must not get taller. It did — tokens wrapped onto a second line and pushed the date control down with them.
  • It should widen instead, as filters are added.
  • Once it hits its width limit, the extras go into a dropdown.
  • Removing one lets it shrink back gradually, not snap.

The funnel, the column list and the tick-lists behave exactly as they do in the app today — only what the bar does with the filters is new.

3 · The rule

  • Height is fixed at 34px. min-height and max-height are both set and tokens never wrap, so nothing below the toolbar can be pushed down.
  • Width is animated. The bar is given an exact pixel width whenever the filters change, over 0.22s. Adding grows it, removing shrinks it — the same motion in reverse.
  • The limit is the room actually left on the toolbar, measured, not a fixed number — so it behaves on a wide monitor and a small laptop alike, and the date control never moves.
  • Past the limit the newest filters fold into a +N chip; press it and they are listed, each with its own ×.
  • Removing one pulls a hidden one back in, so the bar is always as full as it can be.

4 · The one thing you have to choose

Add three or four filters above and you will see it: the bar folds almost immediately. That is not the bar being mean — it is arithmetic. On a 1500px screen the toolbar has about 1240px. The tabs take ~406 and the date control ~374, which leaves the bar ~446px, and 446px holds the icon, the typing space, the funnel and about two filters.
